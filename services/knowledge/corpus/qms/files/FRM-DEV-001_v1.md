## Instructions
Complete this form within 1 business day of discovering a deviation, as required by SOP-DEV-001.

## Fields
- Procedure and version deviated from.
- Planned or unplanned.
- Description of what happened, without patient identifiers.
- Affected runs, jobs or libraries by their core identifiers.
- Immediate containment taken.
- Proposed severity: minor, major or critical.
- Reporter role and date.

## QA Section
- Assigned severity and owner.
- Root cause summary for major and critical deviations.
- Linked CAPA and effectiveness check date.
