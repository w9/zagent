## Instructions
Complete one record per library reviewed under SOP-QC-001 and attach it to the run record.

## Fields
- Run ID and library identifier.
- Application and confirmed min_coverage.
- Observed mean coverage, mapping rate and duplication rate.
- Tumor purity estimate and whether the low-purity warning applies.
- Decision: pass, hold for review, or top-up sequencing requested.
- Reviewer name, role and date.

## Notes
Duplication above 15 percent is recorded in the comments field.
A hold decision links the incident or deviation that explains it.
