## 1. Scope
This addendum applies only to specimens accessioned at Lakeside Helix Hospital.
It adds site steps to SOP-ACC-001 and does not replace it.

## 2. Courier Cutoff
The Lakeside courier leaves for the core at 14:00 local time on weekdays.
Specimens accessioned after the cutoff are stored at 4 degrees and sent the next morning.

## 3. Labels
Lakeside requisitions use the hospital's own MRN format; enter it exactly as printed.
Lakeside RNA specimens are collected in stabilizing tubes and must not be frozen before shipment.

## 4. Contacts
Questions go to the Lakeside lab supervisor through the service desk queue LAKESIDE-INTAKE.
