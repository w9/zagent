## 1. Purpose
This procedure covers receipt, accessioning and labeling of specimens sent to the core.

## 2. Receipt
Specimens are logged in the LIMS on the day of arrival.
Each tube must carry two identifiers that match the requisition.

## 3. Specimen Roles
The specimen role is recorded as reported on the manifest and corrected in place when an error is found.

## 4. Labeling
Print barcode labels from the LIMS.
