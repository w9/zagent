## 1. Purpose
This procedure covers receipt, accessioning and labeling of specimens sent to the core.

## 2. Receipt
Specimens are received at the site intake bench and logged in the LIMS within 2 hours of arrival.
Each tube must carry two identifiers that match the requisition: the patient MRN and the collection date.
Specimens with mismatched or missing identifiers are quarantined and the sending team is called.

## 3. Specimen Roles
The specimen role (tumor, normal or RNA) is recorded as reported on the manifest.
Assay-verified identity is recorded separately after the genotype identity check in SOP-IDENTITY-001.
Never overwrite the reported role; the verified identity field is the correction mechanism.

## 4. Labeling
Print barcode labels from the LIMS only; handwritten labels are not accepted.
Each specimen receives a core specimen identifier and each derived library receives a library identifier.
Record the pathology purity estimate for tumor specimens when it is available.
