## 1. Purpose
This procedure defines retention periods and privacy rules for sequencing data and records.

## 2. Retention
Raw FASTQ files are kept for 2 years after the run date.
Aligned BAM or CRAM files and VCF files are kept for 10 years.
QC review records and deviation records are kept for 10 years.

## 3. Privacy
Patient identifiers must not be entered into wiki pages, tickets or the knowledge base.
Refer to work by job ID, run ID or a pseudonymous specimen reference instead.
When an identifier is found in a ticket or page, the owner removes it and records a privacy deviation.
Exports to external collaborators require a de-identified data set and a data use agreement.

## 4. Access
Access to raw data follows least privilege and is reviewed every 6 months.
Site-specific records are visible only to that site's staff unless QA grants wider access.
