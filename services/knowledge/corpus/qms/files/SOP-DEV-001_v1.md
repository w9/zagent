## 1. Purpose
This procedure defines how deviations are reported, investigated and closed.

## 2. Reporting
Report a deviation within 1 business day of discovery on FRM-DEV-001.
A deviation is any departure from an effective procedure, planned or unplanned.
Planned deviations need QA approval before the work starts.

## 3. Investigation
The owner assigns a severity of minor, major or critical within 2 business days.
Major and critical deviations require a root cause analysis and a corrective and preventive action.
Link related incident tickets and change requests to the deviation record.

## 4. Corrective and Preventive Action
Each CAPA names an owner, an action, a due date and an effectiveness check.
The effectiveness check is performed 90 days after the action is complete.
A CAPA is closed only by QA after the effectiveness check passes.

## 5. Trending
QA reviews deviations by category every quarter and reports recurring causes to the lab director.
