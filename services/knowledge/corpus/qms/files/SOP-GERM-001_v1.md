## 1. Purpose
This procedure governs germline panel analysis for inherited cancer predisposition.

## 2. Requirements
Germline panels require PCR-free WGS libraries.
Germline panel analysis requires a minimum coverage of 25x on the normal library.

## 3. Panel Content
Predisposition panel genes: TP53, RB1, WT1, NF1, APC, BRCA1, BRCA2, PALB2, MLH1, MSH2, MSH6, PMS2, DICER1, SMARCB1, PTCH1, SUFU, VHL, RET, MEN1, CDKN2A, ALK, PHOX2B.

## 4. Classification
Variant classification is performed by a qualified reviewer.
