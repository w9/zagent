## 1. Purpose
This procedure governs germline panel analysis and reporting for inherited cancer predisposition.

## 2. Requirements
Germline panels require PCR-free WGS libraries prepared under SOP-PREP-001.
Germline panel analysis requires a minimum coverage of 30x on the normal library.
The predisposition panel is the default; the extended panel is selected only on request.

## 3. Panel Content
Predisposition panel genes: TP53, RB1, WT1, NF1, APC, BRCA1, BRCA2, PALB2, MLH1, MSH2, MSH6, PMS2, DICER1, SMARCB1, PTCH1, SUFU, VHL, RET, MEN1, CDKN2A, ALK, PHOX2B.
Extended panel adds: CHEK2, ATM, NBN, BLM, FANCA, MUTYH, SDHB, SDHD, TSC1, TSC2, NF2, SMARCA4, BAP1, RUNX1, GATA2, CEBPA, ETV6, PAX5, IKZF1.

## 4. Classification
Variant classification is performed by a qualified reviewer using the laboratory's interpretation guide.
The workbench never releases a germline result and never contacts patients or families.
Variants of uncertain significance are reported only under the reviewer's signed decision.
