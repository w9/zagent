## 1. Purpose
This procedure governs analysis drafts and their confirmation in the Zagent workbench.

## 2. Drafts
An analysis starts as a draft that names an application, a specimen selection and parameters.
The assistant may create and edit drafts but has no ability to submit them.
Each draft version has a content hash that covers the application, the specimen selection and the parameters.

## 3. Human Confirmation
Only a person can confirm a draft, using the Confirm control on the exact version they reviewed.
A chat reply such as yes is not a confirmation.
If the draft changed after the reviewer opened it, confirmation is rejected and the changes are shown.

## 4. Reruns
A failed job is never edited or restarted in place.
A corrected rerun is a new draft that needs its own human confirmation.

## 5. Records
The confirmation, the draft version and the resulting job are kept together for audit.
