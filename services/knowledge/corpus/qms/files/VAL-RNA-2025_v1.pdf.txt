MERIDIAN CLINICAL GENOMICS CORE - VALIDATION SUMMARY
CONTROLLED COPY - RNA Fusion Detection Validation Summary
Document: VAL-RNA-2025 Version 1 Page 1 of 1

1 SCOPE
This summary covers fusion detection from stranded poly-A RNA
libraries.

2 RESULTS
With a minimum of 5 supporting reads, sensitivity for known
fusions was 94.7 percent.
Raising the minimum to 10 supporting reads lowered
sensitivity to 88.3 percent with no further drop in false
positives.
Most false positives were read-through transcripts, which the
artifact filter removed.

3 CONCLUSIONS
The default min_support of 5 and artifact filtering on are
adopted in SOP-FUS-001.

Printed copies are uncontrolled.
