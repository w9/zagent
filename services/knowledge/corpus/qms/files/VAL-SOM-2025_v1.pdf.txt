Document: VAL-SOM-2025 Version 1 Page 1 of 1

1 SCOPE
This summary covers the limit of detection of somatic-snv
calling on PCR-free WGS tumor and normal pairs.

2 RESULTS
At 40x tumor coverage the limit of detection was 0.05 VAF
with 95 percent sensitivity.
At 30x tumor coverage the limit of detection rose to 0.08
VAF.
Below 0.20 tumor purity, sensitivity for subclonal variants
fell by roughly one third.

3 CONCLUSIONS
The default min_vaf of 0.05 is supported only with at least
40x tumor coverage.
A 45x tumor minimum was modelled and is being evaluated
under CHG-0024.

Printed copies are uncontrolled.
