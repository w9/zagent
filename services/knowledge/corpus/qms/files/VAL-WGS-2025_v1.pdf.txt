1 SCOPE
This summary covers validation of PCR-free whole genome
sequencing for germline panels and somatic calling.

2 DESIGN
Reference materials with known variants were sequenced at
four coverage levels in triplicate.
Somatic performance used dilution series of a tumor cell
line into its matched normal.

3 RESULTS
| Coverage | Germline SNV sensitivity | Somatic SNV sensitivity at 0.10 VAF |
| 25x | 98.2 percent | 84.5 percent |
| 30x | 99.1 percent | 91.2 percent |
| 40x | 99.5 percent | 96.4 percent |
| 60x | 99.6 percent | 98.8 percent |
Germline SNV sensitivity was 99.1 percent at 30x mean
coverage.
Somatic SNV sensitivity at 0.10 VAF rose from 91.2 percent
at 30x to 96.4 percent at 40x.

Printed copies are uncontrolled.

4 CONCLUSIONS
A 30x minimum is adequate for germline panels.
The results support a higher minimum for tumor libraries,
which was adopted later under CHG-0021.

5 LIMITATIONS
Reference materials do not reproduce formalin damage or low
tumor purity.

Printed copies are uncontrolled.
