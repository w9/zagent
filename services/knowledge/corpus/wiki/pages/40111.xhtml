Analyses run in the Zagent workbench as drafts that a person confirms. You can prepare a draft yourself or ask the workbench assistant to prepare one, but only you can confirm it.

Steps

  1. Open a service request in the service desk with the request type Analysis.
  2. Name the application: readqc, somatic-snv, rna-fusion or germline-panel.
  3. Describe the specimens by site, patient study code and intake window. Do not paste MRNs into the request.
  4. Prepare the draft in the workbench and check the validation messages on the form.
  5. Confirm the draft yourself with the Confirm control. A chat reply of yes does not submit anything.

Which application do I need?

QuestionApplication
Is the sequencing deep and clean enough?readqc
Which somatic SNVs and indels does the tumor carry?somatic-snv
Is there a gene fusion in the RNA?rna-fusion
Is there an inherited predisposition variant?germline-panel

Somatic calling needs a tumor and a matched normal from the same patient in one specimen selection. There is no separate matched normal field.

Germline panels need PCR-free WGS libraries. Requests on other library types are returned.
