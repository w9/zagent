Fields on the Analysis request type in the service desk.

FieldRequiredNotes
ApplicationYesOne of readqc, somatic-snv, rna-fusion, germline-panel.
SiteYesThe requesting site; records are visible to that site.
Specimen descriptionYesStudy code and intake window, never an MRN.
ParametersNoLeave empty to use defaults from the analysis SOP.
JustificationSometimesNeeded to turn off fusion artifact filtering or to lower min_vaf.
PriorityNoUrgent requests need a lab director approval comment.

Requests missing a required field are sent back with the status Waiting for requester.
