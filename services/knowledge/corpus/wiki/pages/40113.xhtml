Do internal network sites pay for analyses?

No. Network sites are funded centrally. Charges apply only to external research collaborators.

What does a top-up cost?

Top-up sequencing after a coverage failure is charged to the core's quality budget, not to the requester.

Who approves an exception?

The lab director approves fee exceptions. Ask in the service request; do not email the director directly.

Is there a charge for reruns?

Reruns caused by a core error, such as a specimen swap at accessioning, are free.
