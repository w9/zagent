Germline small variant calling runs inside the germline-panel application. Reads are aligned to GRCh38 and variants are called jointly across the panel regions.

Method

  1. Align reads with the Autopipe mapping step and mark duplicates.
  2. Recalibrate base qualities against known sites from the reference library.
  3. Call SNVs and indels in the panel regions plus 20 base pairs of padding.
  4. Annotate with the transcript set listed in the reference library.

Outputs

The job writes a VCF restricted to panel regions and a coverage summary per gene. Genes with less than 20x coverage over any exon are flagged for review.

Classification and reporting follow SOP-GERM-001. This page describes calling only.
