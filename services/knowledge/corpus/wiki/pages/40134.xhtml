Somatic SNV and indel calling compares a tumor library with a matched normal library from the same patient. The application is somatic-snv.

Inputs

  • One specimen selection that contains both the tumor and the normal specimen of the same patient.
  • Tumor libraries with at least 40x mean coverage, as required by SOP-QC-001 version 3 since 2025-11-03.
  • A passing genotype concordance check between tumor and normal.

Parameters

ParameterDefaultMeaning
min_vaf0.05Lowest variant allele frequency reported.
min_depth20Lowest tumor read depth at the variant position.

Defaults come from SOP-VAR-001. Lowering min_vaf below 0.05 needs a justification and at least 100x tumor coverage.

When a job fails

A genotype_concordance_failed code means the tumor and normal roles in the selection do not match the verified identity. Follow SOP-IDENTITY-001 and prepare a new draft with verified roles.

A low_purity_warning does not fail the job. Sensitivity for subclonal variants is reduced below 0.20 purity.
