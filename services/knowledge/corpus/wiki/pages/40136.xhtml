Germline QC checks that a normal library is fit for germline panel analysis before variants are called.

Checks

CheckExpected
Mean coverageAt least 30x
Mapping rateAt least 95 percent
Heterozygous to homozygous ratio1.4 to 2.2 for whole genomes
Reported sex matches inferred sexYes
Contamination estimateBelow 2 percent

A sex mismatch or contamination above 2 percent is treated like an identity problem and escalated to the quality team.
