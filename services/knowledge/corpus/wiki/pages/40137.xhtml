Before somatic calling, the pipeline compares tumor and normal genotypes at a panel of common SNPs to confirm both specimens come from the same person and that their roles are right.

How it works

Each specimen is genotyped at the identity panel sites. Concordance is the fraction of sites where tumor and normal agree. SOP-IDENTITY-001 requires at least 0.90.

Typical causes of failure

  • A tumor/normal swap at accessioning, so the roles are reversed. People also call this a T/N swap or a sample mix-up.
  • Two different patients' specimens were selected together.
  • A heavily contaminated normal.

What to do

Do not edit the clinical specimen records. Select the patient's specimens with verified identity, prepare a new draft and have a person confirm it. The failed job stays in the record.
