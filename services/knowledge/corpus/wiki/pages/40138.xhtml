Two germline panels are offered through the germline-panel application. The panel parameter selects one of them.

PanelGenesWhen to use
predisposition22Default for children with cancer.
extended41Family history of cancer or a research protocol that asks for it.

The gene lists are maintained in SOP-GERM-001 and copied into the reference tables. This page is not the source of truth for gene content.

Both panels need PCR-free WGS libraries with at least 30x on the normal library.
