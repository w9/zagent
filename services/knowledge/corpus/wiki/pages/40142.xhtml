The rna-fusion application detects gene fusions from split and spanning reads in stranded RNA libraries.

Parameters

ParameterDefaultMeaning
min_support5Minimum supporting reads, split plus spanning.
filter_artifactstrueRemoves read-through and other known artifact classes.

Turning off artifact filtering needs a justification in the service request. Expect many read-through calls without it.
