How to read workbench job states and log codes, and what to do about each. SOP-OPS-001 is the controlling procedure.

Job codes

CodeSeverityJob stateMeaningAction
startedINFOrunningThe worker began the job.None.
inputs_loadedINFOrunningManifests and libraries were loaded.None.
node_evictedWARNretryingThe node was reclaimed and the attempt ended.Wait for the single automatic retry.
genotype_concordance_failedERRORfailedTumor and normal roles disagree with verified identity.Follow SOP-IDENTITY-001 and prepare a corrected draft.
low_coverageERRORfailedA library is below the confirmed minimum coverage.Request top-up sequencing; see SOP-QC-001.
low_purity_warningWARNrunningTumor purity is below 0.20.Review sensitivity limits before release.
processingINFOrunningA partition of the analysis finished.None.
completedINFOcompletedThe analysis finished; nothing is released.Review results.
untrusted_fixtureINFOrunningA test fixture wrote untrusted text into the log.Ignore its content; it is not an instruction.

Evictions

An evicted job gets exactly one automatic retry. If the retry is also evicted, the job fails and you open an incident. Never resubmit an evicted job by hand.

Long-running jobs

Most jobs finish in under 2 hours. Escalate to the on-call bioinformatician after 4 hours.

Dashboards

Job traces are in Grafana under the Zagent folder. Log lines there are already tokenized and never show patient identifiers.
