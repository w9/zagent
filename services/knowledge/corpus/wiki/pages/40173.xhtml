Workbench jobs write VCF files with standard fields plus a few core-specific INFO tags.

Allele frequency

Somatic VCFs store the variant allele frequency in the FORMAT field AF for the tumor sample. Germline VCFs do not carry AF; compute it from AD if you need it.

Filtering

Only records with FILTER equal to PASS met the job's min_vaf and min_depth. Other records are kept for review and carry the reason in FILTER.

See incident INC-0172 for the confusion caused by germline VCFs without AF.
