The core talks about data in levels. Higher levels are smaller and closer to interpretation.

LevelContentsExample
Level 1Raw instrument outputFASTQ
Level 2Aligned readsCRAM
Level 3Calls and measurementsVCF, fusion table, coverage summary
Level 4Reviewed, interpreted resultsSigned report

The workbench produces level 3 data. Level 4 results are created only by a qualified reviewer.
