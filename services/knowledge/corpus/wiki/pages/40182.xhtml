RefSeq accessions tell you how a transcript record was made.

  • NM_ records are curated mRNA transcripts and are preferred for reporting.
  • XM_ records are predicted by the annotation pipeline and can change between releases.
  • NR_ and XR_ are their non-coding counterparts.

The core reports variants on MANE Select transcripts, which pair one NM_ record with one Ensembl transcript.
