VAF, the variant allele frequency, is the fraction of reads at a position that carry the variant. Some tools call it allele fraction or AF.

A heterozygous germline variant has a VAF near 0.5. A somatic variant's VAF depends on tumor purity, copy number and whether the variant is clonal.

Worked example

With 100 reads at a position and 12 reads showing the variant, the VAF is 0.12.

In a tumor with 0.40 purity, a clonal heterozygous somatic variant in a diploid region is expected near 0.20 VAF.

In the workbench

The somatic-snv parameter min_vaf sets the lowest VAF that is reported. The default is 0.05 under SOP-VAR-001.
