Tumor purity is the fraction of cells in a tumor specimen that are cancer cells. People also say tumor cellularity or tumor content. Ploidy is the average number of chromosome copies in those cells.

Why it matters

Low purity dilutes somatic variants, so their VAF drops and some fall below min_vaf. Below 0.20 purity the workbench raises a low_purity_warning, as set in SOP-QC-001.

Where estimates come from

  • Pathology review at accessioning, recorded in the specimen record.
  • Computational estimates from copy number and VAF patterns after sequencing.

When the two estimates disagree by more than 0.20, mention it in the review.
