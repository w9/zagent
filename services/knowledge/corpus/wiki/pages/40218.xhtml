Why did my somatic job fail on identity?

The tumor and normal in your selection disagree with verified identity. Check for a T/N swap and prepare a corrected draft with verified roles. See SOP-IDENTITY-001.

What VAF cut-off do we use?

The default min_vaf is 0.05. You can go to 0.03 only with 100x tumor coverage and a written reason.

Do I need a matched normal?

Yes. Select the tumor and the normal of the same patient together. There is no separate matched normal parameter.

Does a purity warning mean the job failed?

No. A low_purity_warning is a warning on a completed job. Review sensitivity before release.

How deep should tumors be sequenced?

Plan for at least 40x so the tumor passes readqc on the first attempt.
